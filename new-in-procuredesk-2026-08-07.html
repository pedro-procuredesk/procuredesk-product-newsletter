📜 Request for Contract  ·  New

Contract requests, out of email and into the workflow

Employees raise a contract request in ProcureDesk, it routes through the approvals you already use, and the approved request turns itself into a live Contract.

Today, contract requests happen entirely over email. Employees message Legal or Procurement directly, document versions get lost in the thread, and there's no way to check where a request stands without chasing someone down. Request for Contract moves the whole exchange into ProcureDesk: the employee submits the request with supporting documents attached, and it routes through your existing approval workflow automatically. Once it's approved, the requester uploads the signed document and ProcureDesk creates the live Contract record on its own.

Why it matters

No manual conversion, no follow-up needed — an approved request doesn't sit around waiting for someone to re-enter it as a contract.

A status anyone can check
Draft → Pending Review → Approved → Contract Live, visible without messaging anyone.
  
Version-tracked documents
Supporting docs stay attached to the request, so nobody is guessing which version is current.
Your existing approval engine
No new workflow to build — contract requests run on the routing rules you've already set up.
  
Auto-creates the Contract
Upload the signed document and the live Contract record is created for you.

Purchase Orders · Procurement

Vendor Collaboration for Orders

Message vendors right on the purchase order — and keep every reply attached to it.

PO-related vendor conversations — acknowledgements, order changes, follow-ups — have lived entirely in email, disconnected from ProcureDesk and hard to track. Vendor Collaboration, already available for invoices, now extends to purchase orders: send a message to a vendor right from the order and a real email goes out on your behalf. When the vendor replies, their response is captured automatically and shown on the order itself.

Before
Acknowledgements and change requests scattered across inboxes, with nothing linking them back to the order.
  
Now
One thread on the order. Replies come back automatically, so you never jump into your inbox to keep it going.
A set of default templates ships with it, so the messages you send most often take a few seconds.

Suppliers · Invoices

Supplier W9 Compliance Check

No W9 on file, no invoice through — enforced by ProcureDesk instead of by memory.

Paying a US supplier without a valid W9 on file is a real compliance risk — but until now, nothing in ProcureDesk stopped that invoice from going through anyway. Turn on this new setting and ProcureDesk closes the gap.

The check runs twice

1 The moment you pick the supplier — so you know before you've filled in the rest.
2 At submission — if there's still no W9 on file, the invoice is blocked, with a clear message explaining why.

Expenses · Receipts

Bulk Receipt Reminders

Chase missing receipts while the expense is still a draft — not at submission time.

Draft expense reports with missing receipts used to have just one enforcement point: a hard block at submission. There was no way for a manager or admin to proactively nudge someone about a missing receipt before then.

→ Select in bulk — pick one or more draft expenses straight from the Expenses list.
→ Send from bulk actions — the reminder goes out from the same menu you already use for bulk operations.
→ One email, one prompt — the expense contact gets a single email asking them to upload what's missing.
Nothing sits around waiting until month-end because nobody remembered to ask.

Single Sign-On for Mobile

The SSO login your team already uses on the web now works in the mobile app.

ProcureDesk mobile app

Single Sign-On has been available on ProcureDesk's web app for a while now, giving your team one less password to worry about — and starting with this release, that same convenience comes to the mobile app. Employees log into ProcureDesk on their phones using your company's existing SSO provider, the exact same way they already do on the web, so there's no separate ProcureDesk password to create, remember, or reset.

The same secure, familiar login your team is used to — now wherever they're working from.
