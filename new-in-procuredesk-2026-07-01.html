● Feature Spotlight
✨ AI Classification  ·  Beta

Your invoices, coded automatically

AI fills in the Account, Class, and other fields on non-PO invoice lines — so your team reviews instead of retypes.

Invoices › Line Items › Show AI suggestions

Coding non-PO invoice lines is the same work on every invoice: pick the account, pick the class, fill the fields, then do it again on the next one — and across high invoice volumes it adds up fast. AI Classification does that first pass for you, and it learns from your company's own coding patterns, so it gets smarter the more you use it. Every suggestion comes with a confidence score, so your team trusts the safe ones at a glance and gives the low-confidence ones a second look — and applying one is a single click, no retyping and no dropdown.

Why it matters

One of the fastest ways to cut AP workload — without adding headcount. It's in beta now, and getting sharper with every invoice processed.

Confidence on every line
Each suggestion shows how sure the AI is — trust the high ones at a glance, double-check the low ones.
  
One-click apply
Accept a suggestion with a single click — no retyping, no hunting through a dropdown.
Learns your patterns
Trained on your own coding history, so accuracy climbs with every invoice processed.
  
Built for volume
If your team codes a lot of non-PO invoices, this is the fastest place to win time back.

Invoices · AP

Vendor Collaboration for Invoices

Message vendors right on the invoice — and keep every reply attached to it.

When AP chases a vendor over email and spreadsheets, the thread lives away from the invoice — so context gets lost, disputes are hard to see, and no one can tell who said what. Vendor Collaboration moves the whole exchange onto the invoice itself, and replies are captured and mapped back to the right invoice automatically.

Before
Email threads and spreadsheets, disconnected from the invoice. Lost context, no clear record of who said what.
  
Now
One thread, on the invoice. Replies come back tagged and the full history stays put — no digging through inboxes.

Mobile · Invoices

Invoice Creation from the Mobile App

A paper invoice becomes a ProcureDesk draft — straight from your phone.

When a paper invoice arrives and the person who has it is away from their desk, it normally waits until they're back on the web app — and that wait slows the whole invoice down. Now it goes in on the spot, and picks up the normal workflow from there.

1 Snap or upload the invoice in the mobile app.
2 ProcureDesk reads it and builds a draft invoice — no manual entry.
3 Finish on the web — code, review, and submit as usual.
The draft is on the web the moment you're back at your desk — captured, not re-typed.

Pay · Invoices

Invoice Aging Dashboard — Enhancements

Your aging report can finally act — filter what's due, then pay it, without leaving the page.

Pay › Invoices › Aging   Live now · nothing to enable

The old dashboard reported aging in a table, then left you to go somewhere else to act on it. Now two interactive widgets sit above the table, and what needs attention is one click from being handled.

Unpaid invoices, grouped by due date

Overdue  0–7 days  7–14 days  14–30 days  30+ days

→ Aging Buckets — the total payable sitting in each due-date bucket above.
→ Supplier Exposure — your top suppliers by unpaid amount, scoped to the bucket you pick.

Click any bucket or supplier and the table filters instantly, with both widgets staying in sync. Combine them for something like "Amazon, due in the next 7 days."

Act without leaving the page

Select the invoices you've filtered to and mark them paid, or build a payment batch — right there, no switching screens.
