🧾 Expenses  ·  Updated

Expense entry, rebuilt around how people actually fill it in

Mileage gets its own tab with the math done for you, regular expenses are grouped into readable sections with the receipt alongside, and editing a multi-line expense no longer means a pop-up for every single line.

Creating and editing expenses has always worked, but it asked for more clicks than it needed to. Mileage sat in the same form as everything else. A new expense opened without a line ready to type into. And editing an expense with several lines — the kind created from draft transactions — meant opening a pop-up, saving, closing it, and opening the next one, line after line. This release reworks all three.

Why it matters

The whole change is aimed at one thing — expense entry and editing that's faster and less repetitive for the person submitting it.

Mileage on its own tab
Enter the locations and the distance, rate, and total amount are calculated for you.
  
Sections you can scan
Expense details, accounting details, additional details, and budget — with the receipt shown prominently alongside.
The first line opens ready
Create a new expense and the first line is already open for editing — no extra click to get started.
  
Save and continue
On a multi-line expense, saving a line takes you straight into the next one.
Before
Close and reopen a pop-up for every line you wanted to change.
  
Now
Click into a line, make the change, hit “Save and continue” — and you're already in the next one.

Contracts · Repository

Create Contracts from Uploaded Documents

Upload the contract document and the draft populates itself — instead of you keying the details in from scratch.

Adding an existing contract to the repository has meant reading the document on one screen and re-typing what it says into another. This release adds a From Document option under Create Contract, alongside the manual flow you already know. You upload the live contract and ProcureDesk extracts the key details automatically — the same way invoice data is extracted today.

How it goes

1 Pick From Document — a new option under Create Contract, next to manual creation.
2 Upload the live contract — the same document you'd otherwise be reading the details off.
3 The fields come back filled — supplier number, contract number, contract name, contract type, and more.
4 Review and submit — correct anything that needs correcting, then submit.
You go from data entry to review — reading what's already there instead of typing it in.

Contracts · Purchase Orders

Archive & Restore Contracts

Take contracts you're not renewing out of circulation — without deleting them, and with the reason on the record.

A contract you've decided not to renew stays live in ProcureDesk until someone does something about it — which means it stays selectable when a colleague links a contract to a purchase order. Archive closes that gap. Any contract in Live status can now be archived with a reason (“will not be renewed”, for example), and it drops out of the contract dropdown on POs straight away.

Before
Contracts nobody plans to use again sit in the dropdown, waiting to be picked by mistake.
  
Now
Archived contracts stop appearing on the PO — and come back to Live whenever you need them.
→ Archiving asks why — a reason is captured every time, so the decision isn't lost with the person who made it.
→ Restoring is one action — archived contracts go back to Live at any time, with a reason captured there too.
→ Nothing is deleted — the contract record stays where it is, out of the way rather than gone.

On the audit trail

Every status change — live to archived, archived to live — is recorded with who made it, when, and why. The contract's full history reads back in one place.

Invoices · Accounts Payable

Real-Time Duplicate Invoice Detection

Duplicates are flagged the moment you enter the invoice number — not after you've filled out the whole form.

Catching a duplicate invoice after submitting it means undoing work you've already done. Now the check runs on the invoice number as you type it, so you know before you go any further — and it looks beyond exact matches too.

What the check does

1 Catches exact duplicates as you type — enter the invoice number and you find out right away, rather than after submitting.
2 Catches near-duplicates too — the kind where a supplier resends the same invoice with a small change to the number.
3 Lets you mark it on the spot — a popup shows the potential duplicate and you mark it there, without digging up the original to compare.

⚙️ New setting — Potential Duplicate Similarity Threshold

You decide how strict the near-duplicate check is. Tighten it if you only want the obvious cases; loosen it if you'd rather see more and dismiss what isn't.

Fewer duplicate payments — and less time spent catching them after the fact.
